About us

Heal naturally, live better.

A wellness centre built around listening first and treating the whole person.

Our approach

Holistic, personal, unhurried.

At Amrutha Acu Healing we see acupuncture as one part of caring for the whole person. A visit starts with a conversation about what you feel, how you sleep, how you move and what is going on in your life.

From there, treatment is personalised. We aim for a calm, comfortable setting where you can ask questions, take your time and feel looked after.

Acupuncture is a complementary therapy. It works alongside, and does not replace, medical diagnosis and care from your doctor.
