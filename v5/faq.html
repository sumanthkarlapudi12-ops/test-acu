Common questions

Good to know

Straight answers about booking, treatment and what to expect.

Before your visit

What is acupuncture?

Acupuncture is a traditional therapy in which fine needles are placed at specific points on the body. People often use it alongside other care for pain, stress, sleep and general wellbeing.

How do I book?

Call 9642328793, message us on WhatsApp, or use the booking form on this website. Tell us your preferred branch and what you would like help with.

What should I bring?

Any previous medical reports or scans, and a list of medicines you take. Wear loose, comfortable clothing.

Do I need to tell you about my health history?

Yes. Please mention medical conditions, medicines, allergies and pregnancy so your treatment can be planned safely.

During treatment

Is acupuncture painful?

Most people feel little more than a light sensation. Your practitioner will explain what to expect, and you can speak up at any time.

How long is a session?

Please ask when you book. Your practitioner will confirm the length for your visit.

Are the treatments safe?

Acupuncture is generally considered safe when done by a trained practitioner. We use a careful, personalised approach, and you should tell us about anything that may affect your care.

After treatment

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on you and your concern. Your practitioner will discuss a suggested plan after your first consultation, and review it as you go.

Will it replace my medicines or doctor's advice?

No. Acupuncture is a complementary therapy. Please continue medical care and follow your doctor's advice.

Practical

Which branch should I visit?

Any of our three branches: Vijayawada (Loyola), Guntur (Madhura Nagar) or Pamarru (Gandhi Nagar). Choose the one closest to you.

What are your timings and fees?

Please call or message us for current timings and fees. They are confirmed when you book.
