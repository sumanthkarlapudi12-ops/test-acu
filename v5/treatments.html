Pain management

Whatever the source, persistent pain deserves attentive care. We start by understanding your story: where it hurts, when it started and what makes it better or worse.

What a visit may involve

  • A detailed first consultation
  • Treatment personalised to you
  • Regular review of how you are responding

Stress & anxiety relief

Sometimes the most valuable part of a session is the quiet. Many people find acupuncture a calming break for body and mind.

What a visit may involve

  • A peaceful, unhurried setting
  • Relaxing points chosen for you
  • Space to talk about what is on your mind

Migraine & headache care

Frequent headaches and migraines can wear you down. Acupuncture is a gentle, drug-free option many people explore for headache support.

What a visit may involve

  • A conversation about triggers, sleep and routine
  • Fine, comfortable needling at selected points
  • A follow-up plan to track how you feel
Book for this

Back pain

Ongoing back discomfort can limit work and rest. Your practitioner will look at what may be behind it and plan care that suits you.

What a visit may involve

  • Understanding where and when it hurts
  • Treatment focused on the back and surrounding areas
  • A realistic plan for how many visits may help
Book for this

Neck, shoulder & joint pain

Long hours at a desk, age or strain can leave the neck, shoulders and joints stiff. Treatment is tailored to the area and your daily movement.

What a visit may involve

  • Assessment of the painful area and movement
  • Targeted points to ease tension and discomfort
  • Simple guidance you can use at home
Book for this

Knee pain

Knee discomfort affects stairs, walking and sleep. Care aims to support comfort and everyday movement.

What a visit may involve

  • A look at how the knee feels in daily activity
  • Local and supporting points
  • A plan that fits your recovery pace

Sciatica

Pain that travels from the lower back through the hip and leg can be hard to live with. Acupuncture is one supportive option people try.

What a visit may involve

  • Mapping where the pain travels
  • Points along the back, hip and leg
  • Guidance on posture and movement

Sleep disorders support

When rest does not come easily, everything feels harder. A calming approach may support better sleep.

What a visit may involve

  • Talk through sleep habits and stressors
  • Relaxing treatment points
  • Simple evening routine tips

Improved energy & overall wellness

For when you are not unwell, just not yourself. Wellness sessions focus on balance and renewal.

What a visit may involve

  • A whole-body conversation
  • Balancing treatment
  • Check-ins to keep you on track

Menstrual disorders

Private, respectful care for menstrual discomfort and irregularities, discussed openly with your practitioner.

What a visit may involve

  • A confidential conversation about your cycle
  • Treatment personalised to your pattern
  • Follow-up across cycles to see how things change

Before you book

Good to know

  • Tell your practitioner about any medical conditions, medicines, allergies or pregnancy.
  • Bring previous medical reports or scans if you have them.
  • Wear loose, comfortable clothing and eat something light beforehand.
  • For sudden, severe or worsening symptoms, see a doctor first.
