Good to know

Common questions

Is acupuncture painful?

Most people feel little more than a light sensation. Your practitioner will explain what to expect and you can speak up at any time.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on you and your concern. Your practitioner will discuss a suggested plan after your first consultation.

Do I need to prepare?

Wear comfortable clothing, eat something light beforehand, and bring any previous medical reports.

Which branch should I visit?
